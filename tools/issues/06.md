If someone puts a figure from kosmos in a paper or an exam, they need to be able to say where it came from and get the same thing back a year later.

Every export carries a short stamp:

- the exact input, canonicalised
- a hash of that input
- the version of kosmos and of the method used for each computed quantity
- for computed geometry, the force field and the convergence criterion

In SVG and PNG as a discreet footer line; in MOL, SDF, XYZ and PDB as comment records; in CSV and JSON as fields.

The point is falsifiability: given the stamp, anyone can rerun the same version and check that the numbers come out the same. This is the same promise the Lean corpus and `cas/expected.txt` already make, extended to the chemistry.
