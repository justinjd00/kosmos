The layout assumes a desktop with a wide sidebar. On a phone it is unusable and on a tablet it is cramped, which rules out the two devices most likely to be in a student's hand or on a lab bench.

- Under 900px the sidebar becomes a **drawer** — swipe or a button, the canvas keeps the whole screen.
- Pinch to zoom, two-finger pan, long-press for the action that a right-click gives on desktop.
- Hit targets at least 44px. The current chips and swatches are well under that.
- The plot and the 3D view must not fight the page for scroll. Vertical drag inside the canvas pans the plot; outside it scrolls the page; the boundary has to be unambiguous.
- Landscape on a phone is the real reading mode for a graph — treat it as a first-class layout, not a squashed portrait one.
- Test on an actual touch device, not a desktop browser with touch emulation. They disagree about exactly the things that matter.
