Show the 3D structure in the browser and let it be turned, without pulling in a 3D library.

- Ball-and-stick and space-filling, switchable. CPK colours from `element::colour`, radii from `covalent_pm` and `vdw_pm`.
- Depth ordering by painter's algorithm, with a subtle depth cue so the front of the molecule reads as nearer.
- Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset. Same interaction as the Chaos module so the two feel like one program.
- Bonds drawn as two half-cylinders in the colours of their two atoms; double and triple bonds visibly so.
- Hydrogens hideable, because most of the time they are noise.

Canvas 2D with sorted primitives is enough at this scale and keeps the dependency count at zero. If it turns out not to be, WebGL is the fallback — but measure first.
