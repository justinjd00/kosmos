The last of the four original modules, and the one closest to what the Fields grid already does.

- **Turing patterns** — Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion. Spots, stripes, mazes, and the parameter map that says which you get.
- **Predator and prey** — Lotka-Volterra in space, so the cycles become travelling waves.
- **Epidemics** — SIR on a grid and on a network, with the basic reproduction number as a slider.
- **Cellular automata** — Life, and the elementary 1D rules with their space-time diagrams.

Most of the machinery exists: `core/src/field.rs` already steps a grid from its four neighbours, paints it through a palette and hands the bytes to the canvas. The reaction-diffusion systems are two coupled fields instead of one.

Tests in the same spirit as the Fields ones — assert the behaviour, not the pixels. Gray-Scott with known parameters must produce spots rather than stripes; SIR must conserve S+I+R; Life must reproduce a glider's period and displacement exactly.
