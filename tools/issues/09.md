`proofs/` currently covers `+ - * ^ sin cos exp` and every composition of them. Those are differentiable everywhere, so `hasDerivAt_eval` needs no side conditions.

`ln`, `sqrt` and `tan` have domain holes. Extending the theorem means:

- the statement gains hypotheses, e.g. `0 < eval a x` for `ln`
- `derive` alone is no longer enough: the domain condition has to be carried alongside the expression
- the corpus generator in `Proofs/Bridge.lean` has to respect those conditions when it picks sample points
- `core/src/calculus.rs` should say where its derivative is valid, not only what it is

Division belongs in the same piece of work — `d/dx (f/g)` needs `g x != 0`.

The `#print axioms` check in CI must keep reporting only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
