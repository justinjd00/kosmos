One page, rebuilt by CI on every release, at a permanent URL. For every quantity kosmos computes: the reference value, the computed value, the deviation, and where the reference came from.

This is the document a QA department, a supervisor, or a reviewer asks for. It is the difference between "a nice website" and "a tool we are allowed to use for work".

## The material already exists

kosmos has been accumulating this without collecting it:

- the Lean corpus in `core/tests/verified-derivatives.txt` — derivatives generated from a machine-checked proof
- `cas/expected.txt` — 47 algebra results, held by both the OCaml module and the Rust engine
- the physics invariants in `core/src/dynamics.rs` and `core/src/field.rs` — energy conservation, orbital periodicity, one slit versus two
- the TPSA reference set in `core/src/chem/polar.rs` — eleven published compounds
- whatever #1 adds for descriptors

The work is to render them as one artefact with a stable shape, not to invent new checks.

## Requirements

- Machine-readable alongside the human page — JSON, versioned, so someone can diff two releases and see exactly what changed numerically.
- Every reference value carries its source. A number without a provenance is not a reference, it is an assertion.
- **Failures are shown, not hidden.** Where kosmos disagrees with a published value, the report says so, by how much, and why. The glucose TPSA episode during development is the model: the published figure a developer half-remembered was wrong, the table was right, and the honest thing was to write down which.
- Linked from the README, from the citation block (#18), and from the interface.
